Jugnu: rebuilding the Retailer app, and the delivery network behind it.

Four B2B retail apps taken from MVP to high-fidelity, a 3-month UX overhaul of the flagship Retailer app, and optimization work on delivery routing and rider incentives.

The context

Jugnu runs a small ecosystem of B2B retail apps, not just one product. Hafsah joined as Product Designer II in April 2022 and worked across four of them — taking each from MVP to high-fidelity, which meant she wasn't just polishing screens but making early, unproven ideas hold up under real usage.

The Retailer app overhaul

The centerpiece of that work was a 3-month UX overhaul of the Retailer app — the tool retailers use to order stock, manage their accounts, and interact with Jugnu directly. Rather than a visual refresh, the overhaul rethought the core flows retailers relied on most, removing the steps that were costing them time without adding value. The redesigned app drove a 60% increase in user acquisition — evidence that the friction in the old version had been quietly costing Jugnu new retailers before they ever got started.

Method: personas, A/B testing, Clevertap

Across all four apps, Hafsah worked from a consistent method rather than reinventing her process each time: building personas to keep design decisions grounded in who was actually using each app, running A/B tests to validate changes before rolling them out fully, and using Clevertap analytics to see how people were behaving inside the product rather than relying on assumptions. That combination — qualitative grounding plus quantitative validation — is what let four apps move quickly without moving blindly.

Four apps, one method: personas to know who you're designing for, A/B tests to prove what works, analytics to catch what you missed.

Delivery-app routing and rider incentives

Hafsah also worked on the delivery side of the business, optimizing how Jugnu's delivery app routed riders and structured their incentives. This meant designing for a different kind of user entirely — riders moving through the day trying to complete deliveries efficiently — and making sure the app's routing logic and incentive structure actually supported that, rather than working against it.

The throughline

Jugnu is where Hafsah's B2B design method shows up at its most systematic: four products, one consistent research-and-validation process, applied to both the retailers ordering stock and the riders delivering it. The Retailer app overhaul is the clearest single result, but the real skill on display is doing that kind of grounded, tested design work repeatably across an entire product portfolio.
