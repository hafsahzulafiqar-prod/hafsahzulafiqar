Overview

  1. Problem

    Pura, PureHealth's consumer-facing super-health app, read as a set of disconnected screens with little visual hierarchy — usable, but not pleasant to use, and not doing enough to keep users retained and satisfied.

  2. Central Question

    How could Pura become one coherent place for a user's health — trustworthy and easy to act on, not just easy to open?

  3. Result

    The redesign improved user satisfaction with Pura by 60%. Alongside it, a cybersecurity dashboard I designed for a mega facility in Abu Dhabi contributed to a 50% increase in revenue tied to the engagement.

The full story

I joined as CX/UI Designer in August 2023 to lead a full UI revamp aimed at a specific, hard-to-fake goal: increasing user retention and satisfaction by making the app actually pleasant to use, not just usable — with handoff-ready documentation for the development team.

Research

I ran the redesign through the Double Diamond framework — discover, define, develop, deliver — rather than jumping straight to screens. In discover and define, I ran focus groups and competitive analysis to understand where the existing app was falling short of what a "super-health" app should feel like. In develop and deliver, I moved through affinity mapping, rapid prototyping, and A/B testing, validating directions against real user behavior before committing to a final design.

Focus groups and competitive analysis to find the problem. A/B testing to prove the fix. Rapid prototyping to get there fast enough to matter.

Design system

A cybersecurity dashboard for Abu Dhabi

Alongside the Pura work, I designed a cybersecurity dashboard for a mega facility in Abu Dhabi — a very different design problem, built for security operators monitoring a large, high-stakes physical facility rather than consumers managing their health. I designed the dashboard to improve incident-response efficiency, giving operators a clearer, faster path from detecting an issue to acting on it. That work contributed to a 50% increase in revenue tied to the engagement.

Dashboard placeholder

Final outcome

Pura and the cybersecurity dashboard don't look alike on the surface — a consumer wellness app and an enterprise security tool for critical infrastructure — but both came from the same instinct: figure out what the person in front of the screen actually needs to do in that moment, and strip the interface down to make that as fast and clear as possible. At PureCS, that instinct scaled from a health app used by thousands to a dashboard used by the handful of people responsible for keeping a facility safe.
