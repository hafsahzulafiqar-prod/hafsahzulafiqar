PureCS: redesigning Pura, and a cybersecurity dashboard for Abu Dhabi.

A Double Diamond redesign of Pura, PureHealth's super-health app, followed by the design of a cybersecurity dashboard for a mega facility in Abu Dhabi.

CX/UI Designer Aug 2023–Apr 2024 Healthcare
60%Improvement in user satisfaction, Pura redesign
50%Revenue increase from the cybersecurity dashboard

The context

PureCS is part of PureHealth, and Pura is its consumer-facing super-health app — the kind of product that tries to be one place for a user's health, not a single-purpose tool. Hafsah joined as CX/UI Designer in August 2023 to lead a redesign aimed at a specific, hard-to-fake goal: making people actually satisfied with using it, not just able to use it.

Applying the Double Diamond

Hafsah ran the redesign through the Double Diamond framework — discover, define, develop, deliver — rather than jumping straight to screens. In the discover and define phases, she ran focus groups to understand where the existing app was falling short of what a "super-health" app should feel like. In develop and deliver, she moved through rapid prototyping and A/B testing, validating directions against real user behavior before committing to a final design rather than relying on internal opinion.

Focus groups to find the problem. A/B testing to prove the fix. Rapid prototyping to get there fast enough to matter.

The result

The redesign improved user satisfaction with Pura by 60% — a broad, structural improvement that reflected the app finally working the way a health app should: coherent, trustworthy, and easy to act on, not just easy to open.

A cybersecurity dashboard for Abu Dhabi

Alongside the Pura work, Hafsah designed a cybersecurity dashboard for a mega facility in Abu Dhabi — a very different design problem, built for security operators monitoring a large, high-stakes physical facility rather than consumers managing their health. The dashboard was designed to improve incident-response efficiency, giving operators a clearer, faster path from detecting an issue to acting on it. That work contributed to a 50% increase in revenue tied to the engagement.

The throughline

Pura and the cybersecurity dashboard don't look alike on the surface — a consumer wellness app and an enterprise security tool for critical infrastructure — but both came from the same instinct: figure out what the person in front of the screen actually needs to do in that moment, and strip the interface down to make that as fast and clear as possible. At PureCS, that instinct scaled from a health app used by thousands to a dashboard used by the handful of people responsible for keeping a facility safe.
