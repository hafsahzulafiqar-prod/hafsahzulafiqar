About

Design that ships, growth that compounds.

I'm a Customer Experience & Design Manager and Product Design Manager based in Dubai, UAE.

I started as a product designer, moved into CX & design leadership, and got promoted into growth & marketing management — a rare hybrid where I design the experience and grow the numbers behind it. That arc played out most visibly at FITT Meals, where I went from redesigning the customer experience to running the growth strategy that followed from it.

I lead with continuous discovery and the Double Diamond framework on the design side — running usability tests, building personas, and mapping opportunity solution trees before committing to a direction. On the marketing side, I think in terms of CRM lifecycle design and growth strategy: the systems that keep customers coming back, not just the campaigns that bring them in once.

My work spans DTC (FITT Meals), B2B SaaS (Kahunas, Kaso, Jugnu), healthcare (PureHealth, PureCS), and ed-tech consulting (Mualim) — across Pakistan and the UAE.
